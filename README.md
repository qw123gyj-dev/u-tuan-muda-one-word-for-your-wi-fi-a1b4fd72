# One Word for Your Wi-Fi

One word that describes my Wi-Fi, judged head to head by an AI judge on funniness and truth. `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output/entry.txt`), checks it is a single word of 1 to 20 letters with no spaces or punctuation, reads it back and verifies the bytes match.
